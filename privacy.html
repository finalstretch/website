Privacy

The short version: we don’t collect your data. The rest of this page explains what that means in practice.

This website

This site is plain HTML and CSS. It sets no cookies, has no analytics, and loads nothing from other companies, not even fonts. The only JavaScript on the site is a small script on the home page that moves the app carousel. It stores nothing and sends nothing, and you can read it in the page source.

When you visit, our hosting provider handles the request the way any web server does; we don’t add tracking of any kind on top.

Our apps

Our apps do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Specifically:

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
  • No servers of ours. We don’t run a backend, so there’s no database of users anywhere.
  • Everything stays on your device. Anything you enter, like a health condition, a medication list, or a saved search, is stored only in the app on your phone. Delete the app and it’s gone.
  • No analytics, no ads, no trackers. The apps contain no advertising or analytics SDKs and send no usage data to us or to anyone else.

On the App Store, our privacy label reads “Data Not Collected.” Once the apps launch and the code is public, you’ll be able to check that claim yourself.

The public data sources

When you search, your query goes directly from your device to the public data source. For Trialhead that’s ClinicalTrials.gov, run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine; for Rxcall it’s the FDA’s public recall data. We are not in the middle and never see your searches.

Like any request on the internet, the data source’s servers receive your query and your device’s IP address, handled under their own privacy policies. We use direct connections so that nobody else, including us, sits in that path.
